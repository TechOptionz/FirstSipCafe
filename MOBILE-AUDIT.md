# Mobile audit — First Sip Cafe

Audited 2026-09-04 against the current working tree (including the uncommitted edits to
`globals.css`, `Nav.tsx` and `slot-images.ts` made at ~12:06–12:15 today). Every page was
loaded headless in Chromium with touch emulation at **320, 375, 390, 414 and 768 px**
(portrait, DPR 2–3) plus a 667×375 landscape check. Pages: `/`, `/menu`, `/reviews`, `/visit`,
`/item/hot-3`, `/item/food-0`, `/item/matcha-2`. Measurements were taken from the DOM
(`scrollWidth`, bounding boxes, computed styles, image natural sizes), not by eye alone.

**Nothing has been changed yet.**

## Totals

| Severity | Count |
|---|---|
| High | 4 |
| Medium | 5 |
| Low | 9 |

Styling approach in this repo: inline `style={{}}` objects in TSX plus `src/app/globals.css`
for hover states, keyframes and the single `max-width: 859px` breakpoint. Proposed fixes add
small classes to `globals.css` written mobile-first (base = phone, `@media (min-width: 860px)`
restores the current desktop look). No `!important`, no new styling system.
